Qloudsoft Solutions — Package Guide 2025–2026 (Plain-text Summary)

Overview
Qloudsoft Solutions offers packaged digital growth services for 12 industry verticals plus cross-industry add-ons. Core services: Social Media Management (SMM), Search Engine Optimization (SEO), content production, LinkedIn marketing, and optional Meta Ads management. Packages are tiered (Basic/Starter/Growth/Premium or similar) with defined platform coverage, deliverables, and monthly pricing ranges. Meta Ads management is an add-on with a 30% management fee; recommended ad spend varies by industry. All prices exclude 18% GST; ad spend is billed separately.
